How much do movers cost in Toronto?

Local moves are commonly priced by time, crew size and truck; long-distance moves may use a flat or route-based quote. Inventory, stairs, elevator timing, walking distance, parking, packing and specialty items all affect cost. OpenMovers provides the applicable pricing method and itemized scope in writing before booking.

Are moving companies licensed and insured in Ontario?

Ontario does not issue a general provincial mover’s licence. Ask a mover for current liability and cargo insurance information, workplace coverage where applicable, and vehicle credentials for the truck used. Building management may also request a Certificate of Insurance. Ask OpenMovers which documents apply to your route and job.

How far in advance should I book?

For end-of-month dates and the busy summer season, three to four weeks is a useful target. Mid-week and mid-month dates may have more flexibility. Last-minute and same-day moves depend on crew, truck, route and building availability.

Do you require a deposit?

Your written quote will state whether a deposit is required, the amount, accepted payment methods and when it becomes refundable or non-refundable. Never send payment until the company, scope, date and policy are clearly identified in writing.

What is your cancellation policy?

The cancellation and rescheduling terms are included with your booking confirmation because they can depend on the service and timing. Review the notice window and any applicable fee before confirming your date.

Do you handle condo and apartment requirements?

Share your building’s service-elevator window, loading instructions, parking rules and required Certificate of Insurance wording as early as possible. OpenMovers will confirm which coordination and documents apply to your move.

What won’t movers transport?

Hazardous and flammable materials, perishables, pets and some plants are commonly restricted. Policies vary by carrier and border route. Carry jewellery, medication, passports, legal documents, keys and irreplaceable valuables personally.

Do you move cross-border to the U.S.?

OpenMovers plans eligible Toronto-to-U.S. household moves. Unaccompanied goods generally require CBP Form 3299 plus a complete inventory. We confirm the carrier arrangement and help organize the moving inventory; the importer is responsible for accurate customs declarations and eligibility.

What happens if something is damaged?

Your written contract should state the carrier-liability option, claims process, exclusions and deadlines. Under released liability commonly used for household moves in Canada, carrier liability may be limited to $0.60 per pound per article—so a 100 lb item could be limited to $60 regardless of replacement cost. Ask what increased or replacement-value options are available for your specific move before signing.

Can an estimate increase?

If an Ontario moving contract includes an estimate, the province says the mover cannot charge more than 10% above it unless you need additional supplies or services and agree to the increase. This general information is not legal advice; review the scope, exclusions and change process in your contract.
